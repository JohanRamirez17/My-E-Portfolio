My perspective

Personal statement

Learning through hands-on work has shaped how I approach challenges and prepare for the future.

My interest in technology has grown through hands-on experience, especially repairing Chromebooks. Diagnosing hardware problems and helping replace damaged components showed me how individual parts work together in a complete system. I’ve also used spreadsheets to organize repair information and track parts, which taught me to work carefully and systematically.

Balancing school, technical projects, and other commitments has helped me strengthen my time management and independence. I break larger tasks into manageable steps, keep track of deadlines, and ask questions when I need to understand a problem more deeply. I value learning from others, collaborating when it helps, and applying what I learn to solve problems.

These experiences have encouraged me to look beyond hardware repair and explore the digital systems that shape everyday life. I’m especially interested in computer engineering, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity, and I hope to keep learning how technology can be designed and used responsibly.
